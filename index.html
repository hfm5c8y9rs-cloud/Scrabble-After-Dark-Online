How to play

Use your physical Scrabble board. Each player gets 12 turns, across three rounds of four turns per player.

  1. Create a game and have the other player join with the six-digit code.
  2. Each player privately selects a 4–8 letter secret word.
  3. The app randomly chooses the first player.
  4. Active player enters a word by tapping its first square, typing the complete word and selecting its direction.
  5. Review score and optional adult bonus, then submit. The challenge appears on both iPads; only the active player can advance.
  6. At the end, both players reveal their secret words. If a player played the word chosen by their partner, they receive 50 points.

First word must cross center. All later words must connect. The app scores premium squares and crosswords, but does not check a dictionary. Challenges are always optional. The fixed-turn variant does not deduct leftover tiles.

Close and reopen: enter the six-digit code again to rejoin. Either player may end the game after confirmation.
