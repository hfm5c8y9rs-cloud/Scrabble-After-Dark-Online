The Board Can Wait




Rules
Standard 15×15 board, seven tiles per player, classic tile values and premium squares. Take turns placing a continuous word that connects to the existing board. First word crosses the center. Valid words and all crosswords are checked against the included English word list. After each successful move, both players see the After Dark challenge. The game draws from 40 redesigned challenges per round. It avoids repeated themes, tracks clothing challenges, and prioritizes varied activities. Some challenges use partner-selected options, private answers revealed together, or synchronized timers. Each player may accept or decline before the challenge. Either decline skips it with no penalty. Timed challenges open a shared countdown after both accept; either player starts it and both devices hear a chime if sound is enabled. The person who played the word continues after the timer finishes or is skipped.
Three rounds of eight total turns each: 24 total turns, 12 per player. Romantic words earn +5, suggestive words earn +10, and matching your partner’s private secret word earns +50 at the final reveal. The eligible-word list is available below the scores. Bonuses apply to the main word played, not crosswords. Consent always comes first; any challenge may be skipped. The live turn timer and each player’s cumulative thinking time exclude challenges. A three-minute pace is suggested, but no points are deducted for time.
