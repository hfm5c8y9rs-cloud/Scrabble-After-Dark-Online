Rules

Standard 15×15 board, seven tiles per player, classic tile values and premium squares. Take turns placing a continuous word that connects to the existing board. First word crosses the center. Valid words and all crosswords are checked against the included English word list. After each successful move, both players see the After Dark challenge. Each player may accept or decline. Either decline skips the challenge without penalty; if both accept, it is mutual. Timed challenges open a shared countdown after both accept; either player starts it and both devices hear a chime if sound is enabled. The person who played the word continues after the timer finishes or is skipped.

Three rounds of eight total turns each: 24 total turns, 12 per player. Romantic words earn +5, suggestive words earn +10, and matching your partner’s private secret word earns +50 at the final reveal. The eligible-word list is available below the scores. Bonuses apply to the main word played, not crosswords. Consent always comes first; any challenge may be skipped.
