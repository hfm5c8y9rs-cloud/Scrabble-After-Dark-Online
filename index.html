Rules

Standard 15×15 board, seven tiles per player, classic tile values and premium squares. Take turns placing a continuous word that connects to the existing board. First word crosses the center. Valid words and all crosswords are checked against the included English word list. After each successful move, both players see the After Dark challenge. Each player may accept or decline. Either decline skips the challenge without penalty; if both accept, it is mutual. The person who played the word continues to the next turn once the decision is settled.

Three rounds of eight total turns each: 24 total turns, 12 per player. After Dark word bonuses and private secret-word rewards apply. Consent always comes first; any challenge may be skipped.
